          5. How to Read the Result

          Model Rank is the framework's original assessment. Management Rank reflects subsequent human judgment. Moving an opportunity does not rewrite the model's original score. This creates a visible distinction between analytical prioritization and executive judgment.

          The model combines strategic fit, challenge relevance, available-data signals, business model, business value, customer value, feasibility, speed, implementation complexity and risk. Scores are directional prioritization aids, not forecasts of ROI.

          V2.1 deliberately uses a transparent rules-based model. A future AI-powered version can generate company-specific opportunities and reasoning while retaining this structured prioritization framework.
