          5. How to Read the Result

          Model Rank is the framework's original recommendation. Management Rank reflects subsequent human judgment. Management adjustments remain visible instead of rewriting the model's original assessment.

          The model combines strategic fit, challenge relevance, available-data signals, business model, business value, customer value, feasibility, speed, implementation complexity and risk. Scores are directional prioritization aids, not forecasts of ROI.

          V2.2 uses a transparent rules-based strategy model. Future versions can combine this framework with generative AI while retaining structured scoring and management oversight.
