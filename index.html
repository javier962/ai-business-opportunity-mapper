          5. How the Scoring Works

          Each opportunity is assessed across six factors: Business Value, Customer Value, Feasibility, Time-to-Impact, Implementation Complexity and Risk. The model also considers the stated business objective, business model, company size and keywords contained in the business challenges.

          The score is a prioritization aid, not a prediction of ROI. A real implementation decision would require validation of data availability, process economics, integration requirements, organizational readiness, regulatory considerations and expected financial impact.

          V2 uses a transparent rules-based strategy model rather than a live generative-AI model. This makes the prioritization logic inspectable. A future version will combine this framework with AI-generated, company-specific opportunity analysis.
